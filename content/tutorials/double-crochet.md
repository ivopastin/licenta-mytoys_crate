# Double Crochet (dc)

The double crochet is twice as tall as a single crochet, creating an airier fabric. In amigurumi, it's used less often for the main body (which needs to be dense) but frequently for ears, wings, decorative ruffles, and accessories.

---

## What You Need

- Yarn and crochet hook
- A foundation chain or existing stitches to work into

---

## Step-by-Step

**1. Yarn over before inserting the hook**
This is what makes double crochet different from single crochet — you wrap the yarn BEFORE entering the stitch. Bring the working yarn over the hook once from back to front.

**2. Insert your hook into the stitch**
Push the hook into the next stitch. You now have three sections of yarn around your hook area.

**3. Yarn over and pull through the stitch**
Wrap the yarn over the hook and draw it through the stitch only. You now have three loops on your hook.

**4. Yarn over and pull through two loops**
Wrap the yarn over again and draw it through only the first two loops on your hook. Two loops remain.

**5. Yarn over and pull through the last two loops**
Wrap once more and draw through both remaining loops. One loop remains — double crochet complete!

---

## Starting a Row or Round with Double Crochet

When starting a new row in double crochet, chain 3 at the beginning. This chain-3 counts as your first double crochet stitch. At the end of a row, slip stitch into the top of the chain-3 to join.

---

## Tips

- **The initial yarn-over is the most common beginner mistake** — it's easy to forget it when you're used to single crochet.
- **Chain-3 turning chains can look untidy.** Some crocheters prefer to chain-2 and work into the first real stitch instead. Both approaches work; pick the one that looks better with your tension.
- **Double crochet is significantly faster** than single crochet since each stitch covers more height. Great for larger flat pieces like blankets or scarves.
- **For amigurumi ears and flat pieces**, double crochet gives a nice drape and softness that single crochet can't achieve.
