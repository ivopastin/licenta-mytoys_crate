# Increase (inc)

An increase adds an extra stitch into a single existing stitch. Increases are how amigurumi shapes grow — working increases in a regular pattern across a round turns a flat circle into a dome, and eventually into a full sphere.

---

## What It Does

Without increases, a round stays the same size forever. By working two stitches into one stitch, you add one stitch to your total count per increase. The classic amigurumi sphere starts like this:

- Round 1: 6 sc in magic ring (6 stitches)
- Round 2: inc in each stitch around (12 stitches)
- Round 3: \*sc 1, inc\* repeat around (18 stitches)
- Round 4: \*sc 2, inc\* repeat around (24 stitches)

Each round with increases adds 6 stitches total (because you started with 6).

---

## Step-by-Step

**1. Identify the stitch to increase in**
Your pattern will say something like "inc" or "2 sc in next st." Both mean the same thing: work two single crochets into one stitch.

**2. Work your first single crochet**
Insert hook into the stitch, yarn over, pull through — then yarn over and pull through both loops. First sc done.

**3. Work a second single crochet into the same stitch**
Without moving to the next stitch, insert your hook back into the exact same stitch and complete another single crochet.

**4. Move on**
You've now placed two stitches where one was. Move to the next stitch per your pattern.

---

## Recognizing the Shape

When you work increases evenly spaced across rounds, the piece will curve into a flat circle. When increases become less frequent and eventually stop, the piece curves upward into a dome. When you then start decreasing (the reverse), it closes into a sphere.

---

## Tips

- **The increase stitch is invisible on the right side** — the two stitches blend naturally. There's no gap or bump.
- **Count every round after increases.** Adding an unintended extra stitch early can compound as rounds grow.
- **Mark your increases** if you keep losing track — place a marker in the second stitch of each increase so you can count how many you've done.
- **Visible increase (inc) vs. invisible increase**: the standard "2 sc in 1 stitch" increase is already quite tidy. Some patterns may specify a "raised increase" for decorative purposes — follow the pattern notes.
